# Reference pictures

What real analog FPV video looks like, to hold the simulator against.

- `lab/`: our own captures, a BETAFPV Air75 flown indoors, received by an HDZero monitor and
  recorded over HDMI. Multipath at short range. See `lab/README.md`.
- `web/`: stills from other people's analog FPV range tests on YouTube (25 mW to 2.5 W, out
  to 11 km) and an analog satellite TV test: clean through grain, sparklies, the colour
  killer, tearing and snow. Third-party works, not ours to redistribute, so only
  `web/SOURCES.md` is in the repository: where each came from, to the second, and what it
  shows. The stills stay local (git-ignored).
